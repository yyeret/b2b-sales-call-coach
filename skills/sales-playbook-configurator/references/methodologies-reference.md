# B2B Sales Methodologies Reference

Use this reference when configuring a team's sales playbook (`sales-playbook-configurator`) or inspecting a call transcript (`sales-call-analyzer`).

## Quick Selection Matrix

| Methodology | Best-Fit Deal Profile | Primary Strength | Classic Failure Mode When Misapplied |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sandler** | SMB to mid-market; consultative sales; 1–3 stakeholders | Eliminates "happy ears," premature pitching, and unpaid consulting via Up-Front Contracts and the Pain Funnel | Feels scripted or cagey if "negative reverse" tactics are used without genuine curiosity |
| **Force Management (Command of the Message)** | Mid-market to enterprise; competitive/complex transformations; \$50K+ ACV | Connects Positive Business Outcomes (PBOs) to Required Capabilities, Metrics, and Defensible Differentiation | Turns into rigid acronym-filling if reps recite value drivers instead of listening to the buyer's language |
| **MEDDIC / MEDDPICC** (incl. John McMahon rigor) | Complex enterprise / multi-stakeholder deals; 3–12+ month cycles; procurement-heavy | Prevents late-stage forecast surprises by enforcing evidence on Economic Buyer, Champion, Decision/Paper Process | Used as a post-hoc CRM checklist rather than a live discovery guide; weak on *how* to create urgency |
| **Value-Based Selling** | High-consideration B2B where ROI or Cost of Inaction justifies price vs. cheaper alternatives | Anchors price to quantified business impact rather than feature lists or discounting | Produces generic "ROI calculators" that buyers don't believe because inputs weren't co-created |
| **Challenger** | Status-quo / "do nothing" is the #1 competitor; buyers don't yet see the cost of their current approach | Reframes buyer thinking with Commercial Teaching, builds constructive tension, and takes control of next steps | Comes across as arrogant lecturing if the rep skips tailoring or talks *at* the buyer instead of diagnosing |
| **Gap Selling (Keenan)** | Problem-centric B2B where buyers jump to features before understanding root cause | Maps **Current State → Future State → The Gap** (technical, business, and emotional impact + root cause) | Turns discovery into an endless interrogation if the rep doesn't connect the gap to next steps |
| **Science of Sales (Mark Roberge)** | Scaling sales teams (5–100+ reps) needing consistent stage gates, coaching, and leading-indicator telemetry | Turns coaching from gut feel into objective, stage-gated scorecards, talk/listen ratios, and 1-habit coaching loops | Over-indexes on activity metrics or rigid scripts if not paired with a strong buyer-conversation methodology |
| **Blair Enns — Four Sales Conversations** | Consultative/expert B2B & agency/services sales; preventing premature pitching | Separates **Probabilistic → Qualifying → Value → Closing** conversations so reps stop pitching/proposing before earning it | Reps treat every call as a pitch or send proposals before aligning on value and investment |
| **Jonathan Stark — Why Conversation** | High-trust advisory / value-priced engagements; fighting "do nothing" | Four zero-pressure questions (**Why this? Why now? Why us? Why not do nothing?**) that let the buyer articulate urgency and value | Asked mechanically like a survey instead of curious, peer-level exploration |

## Methodology Breakdowns (For Configuration & Call Inspection)

### 1. Sandler Selling System
- **Core Premise:** Equal Business Stature (peer-to-peer, never chasing). Qualification happens *before* presenting. Buyer autonomy is explicit ("it's okay to say no").
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:**
  - **Up-Front Contract:** Aligned on purpose, time, agenda, and possible outcomes (including a clean "no") at the start.
  - **Pain Funnel:** Moved from surface symptom → concrete examples → business/financial impact → personal/stakeholder urgency, rather than jumping to product.
  - **Budget & Decision:** Tested willingness/ability to fund and mapped how a decision actually gets made *before* booking a demo/proposal.
  - **Post-Sell / Next Step:** Clear mutual commitment with a date, not "let me know what you think."
- **Anti-Patterns:** Unpaid consulting (answering "how would you do it?" for free before qualifying), "happy ears" (mistaking polite interest for pain), chasing a vague "send me info."

### 2. Force Management (Command of the Message)
- **Core Premise:** Win by articulating **Value Drivers** and **Positive Business Outcomes (PBOs)** tied to **Required Capabilities** and **Metrics**, while setting traps for competitors.
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:**
  - **Before & After Scenarios:** Uncovered the current painful state and co-defined the measurable future state.
  - **Negative Consequences:** Established what happens if they do nothing.
  - **Required Capabilities & Metrics:** Tied capabilities directly to how the buyer will measure success.
  - **Differentiation / Trap-Setting:** Established criteria where your solution uniquely wins without trash-talking competitors.
  - **Proof Points:** Shared a relevant customer story with measurable outcomes.

### 3. MEDDIC / MEDDPICC
- **Core Premise:** Rigorous deal qualification for complex B2B sales. Separate **what we know (evidence)** from **what we hope (inference)**.
- **Key Elements to Score on Calls:**
  - **M — Metrics:** Quantified economic impact (cost saved, revenue gained, risk reduced).
  - **E — Economic Buyer (EB):** Person with final budget/veto authority (met or verified access).
  - **D — Decision Criteria:** Technical, business, and financial criteria used to evaluate options.
  - **D — Decision Process:** Exact steps, stakeholders, and timeline from evaluation to signature.
  - **P — Paper Process:** Legal, security, procurement, infosec steps and lead times.
  - **I — Identify Pain:** Specific, urgent business pain (not mild inconvenience).
  - **C — Champion:** Someone with power and influence who actively sells internally on your behalf (tested, not assumed).
  - **C — Competition:** Who else is in the deal — including "do nothing" or internal build.

### 4. Value-Based Selling
- **Core Premise:** Price is only an issue in the absence of quantified value. Co-create the business case *with* the buyer using their own numbers.
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:** Cost of Inaction quantified; buyer-owned math (numbers from the buyer's mouth, not a canned ROI deck); outcome framing over feature checklists or early discounting.

### 5. The Challenger Sale
- **Core Premise:** High-performing reps **Teach, Tailor, and Take Control** rather than relying on rapport and generic discovery ("what keeps you up at night?").
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:**
  - **Teach (Commercial Insight):** Shared a counter-intuitive insight about the buyer's industry/problem that reframes how they think and leads back to your unique strengths.
  - **Tailor:** Adapted the insight to this specific persona's KPIs.
  - **Take Control:** Pushed back constructively on bad assumptions, maintained pricing/process discipline, and drove a clear next step.

### 6. Gap Selling (Keenan)
- **Core Premise:** No gap, no sale. Buyers don't buy because of your product; they buy to close the gap between their **Current State** and **Desired Future State**.
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:** Mapped Current State (facts + problems + root cause + impact), Future State (desired outcomes), and quantified the Gap before showing the product.

### 7. Science of Sales (Mark Roberge)
- **Core Premise:** Sales execution is measurable, coachable, and stage-gated. Focus on **leading indicators** and **one coaching focus at a time** backed by objective call data.
- **Key Mechanics to Score on Calls:**
  - **Stage Alignment:** Ran the right play for *this* stage (Discovery vs. Demo vs. Close).
  - **Objective Leading Indicators:** Talk-to-listen ratio (~35–45% rep / 55–65% buyer on discovery), open-ended vs. closed questions, 2–3 layers of follow-up depth, explicit next-step commitment booked on the call.
  - **Single-Habit Coaching:** Identify the **single highest-leverage habit** for the rep to practice on the very next call.

### 8. Blair Enns — The Four Sales Conversations (Win Without Pitching)
- **Core Premise:** Stop pitching and writing proposals for people who haven't qualified. Move sequentially through four distinct conversations:
  1. **The Probabilistic Conversation:** Early fit check — is there a real problem we're uniquely suited to solve? Qualify out fast if not.
  2. **The Qualifying Conversation:** Deep discovery of problem, desired state, constraints, and decision reality *before* solutioning.
  3. **The Value Conversation:** Align on the *value* of the outcome and ballpark investment/options *live* before writing a proposal (so the proposal is a confirmation, never a reveal).
  4. **The Closing Conversation:** Finalize terms, address last-mile friction, and get commitment — or close the loop cleanly.
- **On-Call Inspection:** Did the rep stay in the right conversation, or jump straight to pitching/proposing in Conversation 1 or 2?

### 9. Jonathan Stark — The Why Conversation
- **Core Premise:** Before proposing, get the buyer to answer four "Why" questions in their own words so *they* build the case for change:
  - **Why this?** (Why this specific problem/outcome?)
  - **Why now?** (Why is it urgent today vs. six months from now?)
  - **Why us?** (Why our approach vs. alternatives?)
  - **Why not do nothing?** (What happens if nothing changes?)

## Common High-Signal Hybrid Combinations

1. **Enterprise Complex (ACV \$75K+, 4+ stakeholders):** **MEDDPICC** (qualification & deal control) + **Force Management** (value drivers & PBOs) + **Challenger** (commercial teaching) + **Roberge** (stage-gated coaching).
2. **Mid-Market Consultative (ACV \$15K–\$75K, 2–4 stakeholders):** **Sandler** (equal stature, pain funnel) + **Gap Selling / Value-Based Selling** (quantified gap & ROI) + **Roberge** (leading-indicator coaching).
3. **High-Trust / Advisory / Services Sales:** **Blair Enns Four Conversations** (no premature pitching) + **Jonathan Stark Why Conversation** + **Sandler** (clean disqualification).
4. **Founder-Led / Early-Stage (No Formal Playbook Yet):** **Bootstrap from 3–10 Raw Transcripts** + **Sandler / Mom Test** + **Science of Sales** to extract ICP and first discovery guide.
